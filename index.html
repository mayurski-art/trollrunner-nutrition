Core nutrition topics

Performance Plate

Balance carbs, protein, colorful produce, and healthy fats based on workout intensity.

Hydration

Drink to thirst and replace fluids thoughtfully before, during, and after long sessions.

Recovery Timing

Within 1–2 hours post-run, prioritize carbs + protein to support glycogen and repair.

Weekly Consistency

Focus on repeatable patterns over perfect days: meals, sleep, hydration, and snacks.

Guiding principles

  1. Prefer minimally processed foods most of the time.
  2. Match carb intake to training load, not to social media trends.
  3. Keep protein regular across the day (3–5 feedings).
  4. Use supplements as targeted tools, not substitutes for meals.
  5. Choose sustainable habits that fit real life and budget.
